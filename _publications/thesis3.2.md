<h3>Undergraduate Capstone Thesis / Research Project</h3>

<p>
This work represents my undergraduate capstone research project conducted at
<b>
<a href="https://pust.ac.bd/">
Pabna University of Science and Technology
</a>
</b>,
under the Department of Information and Communication Engineering.
</p>

<p>
The project introduces 
<b>CNN-LCA</b>, a lightweight attention-based deep learning architecture
designed for explainable brain tumor classification from Magnetic Resonance
Imaging (MRI) scans.
</p>

<p>
The proposed framework integrates a 
<b>Lite Convolutional Attention (LCA)</b> mechanism to improve feature
representation while maintaining a lightweight architecture suitable for
resource-constrained environments.
</p>

<p>
For model interpretability, the framework incorporates
<b>confidence-guided Grad-CAM</b>, enabling visualization of the image regions
that contribute most strongly to the model's predictions.
</p>

<p><b>Key Contributions:</b></p>

<ul>

<li>
Designed a lightweight CNN-based architecture with an integrated
Lite Convolutional Attention mechanism.
</li>

<li>
Developed an explainability framework using confidence-guided Grad-CAM.
</li>

<li>
Conducted brain tumor classification across four categories:
<b>Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary Tumor, and No Tumor.</b>
</li>

<li>
Performed experimental evaluation using a dataset containing
<b>7,200 brain MRI images.</b>
</li>

<li>
Evaluated classification performance and model efficiency for practical
deployment in resource-constrained clinical environments.
</li>

<li>
Conducted external validation to investigate the generalization capability
of the proposed framework.
</li>

</ul>

<p>
The experimental results demonstrated strong classification performance,
with the proposed CNN-LCA framework achieving a reported accuracy of
<b>98.40%</b>.
</p>

<p>
The project also emphasizes explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI),
providing visual explanations of model predictions through Grad-CAM
visualizations.
</p>
