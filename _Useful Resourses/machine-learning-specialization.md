## 📌 How to Add Your Own Topic

Each topic should have its own `.md` file.

Example:

```text
machine-learning/
│
├── course-1/
│   ├── week-1/
│   │   ├── overview-of-machine-learning.md
│   │   ├── supervised-vs-unsupervised.md
│   │   ├── quiz-supervised-vs-unsupervised.md
│   │   ├── regression-model.md
│   │   ├── quiz-regression-model.md
│   │   ├── gradient-descent.md
│   │   └── quiz-gradient-descent.md
│   │
│   ├── week-2/
│   ├── week-3/
│   └── week-4/
│
├── course-2/
│   ├── week-1/
│   ├── week-2/
│   ├── week-3/
│   └── week-4/
│
└── course-3/
    ├── week-1/
    ├── week-2/
    ├── week-3/
    └── week-4/
```
